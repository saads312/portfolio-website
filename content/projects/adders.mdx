# High-Frequency Adders on Intel Agilex-5

## Overview

Optimized a **2048-bit adder** on Intel Agilex-5, achieving **41% higher frequency** and
**25% less area** by closing timing with TimeQuest STA using retiming and pipelining.

## Results

| Metric | Before | After | Improvement |
|--------|--------|-------|-------------|
| Frequency | 600 MHz | 848 MHz | **+41%** |
| Area (ALMs) | 10.9K | 8.2K | **−25%** |
| Memory LABs | 700 | 0 | **−100%** |
| LAB Utilization | 1,758 | 1,211 | **−31%** |

## Approach

- Automated a sweep of **11 configurations** with Bash scripts that drive Quartus synthesis and
  parse timing reports to pick the best frequency–area tradeoff
- Selected the optimal Kogge-Stone grouping (M=16) from the sweep results
- Applied **retiming and pipelining** to close timing under TimeQuest STA
- Eliminated all memory LAB usage through optimized ALM packing

## Tools
SystemVerilog, Quartus Prime Pro, TimeQuest STA, Bash, Intel Agilex-5
